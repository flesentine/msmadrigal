Ms. Madrigral Support

Ms. Madrigral is a 500-word Spanish vocabulary trainer with retro 8-bit visuals and pronunciation audio.

Help

Tap the chalkboard to reveal the Spanish answer, then tap again to move to the next word. Use Reshuffle to start a new randomized order and Sound On/Off to control pronunciation.

Contact support

Developer/support contact: @flesentine on GitHub

Preferred support channel: open a Ms. Madrigral support issue.

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include passwords, account numbers, or other sensitive information.
