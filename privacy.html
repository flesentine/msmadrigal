Privacy Policy — Ms. Madrigral

Last updated: August 19, 2026.

Ms. Madrigral is a Spanish vocabulary learning app. The iOS app does not require an account and does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

Data collection

The iOS app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, social-login, or third-party marketing SDKs. Vocabulary, game logic, and pronunciation audio are bundled with the app and are intended to work without sending learning activity to a server.

Device permissions

The app does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, health data, or advertising identifiers.

Website hosting

The public support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. As with most web hosting services, the hosting provider may process standard technical request information for security and service operation. That hosting is separate from the offline learning activity inside the iOS app.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Support

For support or privacy questions, visit the support page.
